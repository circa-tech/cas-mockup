Rebuild with scripts/build-offline-basemap.py. Coordinates are rounded to five
decimal places and linework simplified by approximately five metres. Includes
roads, rivers, water polygons, selected land use and settlements within
71.15°W–68.85°W, 28.85°S–26.85°S. It is a regional reference map, not satellite
imagery. Mapping coverage follows the source data and may be incomplete.
